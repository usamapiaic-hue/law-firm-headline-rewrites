Button and Call-to-Action Rewrites

Buttons and calls to action are the smallest words on a law firm website and some of the most important. Vague buttons lose enquiries; overconfident ones can raise advertising-rule problems. Here are common phrases and clearer alternatives.

Instead ofTry
SubmitSend my question
Win your case todayTalk to an attorney about your case
Get the compensation you deserveFind out what your claim may involve
Contact usCall now or book a free consultation
Guaranteed resultsSee how we approach cases like yours
Best lawyer in townMeet the attorney who will handle your case

Rules of thumb

  • Say what happens when someone clicks.
  • Avoid promises about outcomes.
  • Match the button to the page: a landing page for one practice should have one clear action.
